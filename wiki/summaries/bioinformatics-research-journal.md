# Source Summary: Bioinformatics & Computational Genomics Research Journal

- **Path**: `journal/Bioinformatics_Research_Journal.md`
- **Author**: Süleyman Hacızadə
- **Scope**: Multi-year academic log capturing computational biology concepts across epigenomics, transcriptomics, molecular docking, sequence alignment, and quality control.

## Key Research Themes & Methodologies

1. **Transcriptomics & Differential Expression**:
   - Count-based modeling via Negative Binomial distributions in [[DESeq2]] to model biological coefficient of variation (BCV) and overdispersion.
2. **Epigenomics & Protein-DNA Interactions**:
   - Chromatin Immunoprecipitation sequencing ([[ChIP-Seq-Peak-Calling]]) mapping transcription factor binding peaks and histone modifications.
3. **Sequencing Quality Assessment**:
   - Logarithmic error probabilities via [[Phred-Quality-Score]] ($Q = -10 \log_{10} P$).
4. **Heuristic Database Searching**:
   - High-scoring Segment Pair (HSP) discovery and word seeding in [[BLAST-Algorithm-Heuristics]].
5. **Molecular Biophysics & Translation**:
   - Species-specific [[Codon-Usage-Bias]] correlating with tRNA abundance.
   - Gasteiger-Marsili partial charge calculation in molecular docking.
