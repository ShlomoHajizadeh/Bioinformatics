# Human Insulin Crystal Structure (PDB: 1COH)

**PDB 1COH** is a high-resolution ($1.50\text{ \AA}$) X-ray crystallographic structure of recombinant human insulin crystallized in the $P2_12_12_1$ space group.

## Structural Metrics
- **Chains**: Contains Chain A (21 residues) and Chain B (30 residues) linked by interchain disulfide bonds (A7–B7, A20–B19) and an intrachain disulfide bond (A6–A11).
- **Benchmark Distance**: For Chain A, the Euclidean distance between $\text{C}_\alpha(10)$ and $\text{C}_\alpha(21)$ across the $\alpha$-helix is exactly $\approx 13.56\text{ \AA}$.
- **Biophysical Validation**: Exhibits a strong negative correlation ($r = -0.3685$) between isotropic B-factors and residue packing density.
