# Oxford Nanopore Sequencing

**Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT)** provides third-generation single-molecule sequencing through protein nanopores embedded in an electrically resistant polymer membrane.

## Principles of Operation
- Real-time ionic current disruption as native DNA/RNA passes through the pore.
- Long-read sequencing capability (kbp to Mbp scale).
- Direct detection of native base modifications (e.g., 5-methylcytosine, 6-methyladenine) without bisulfite treatment.

## Applications
- Metagenomic pathogen surveillance and rapid outbreak tracking.
- Structural variant resolution and repeat-region assembly.
- Direct RNA sequencing without reverse transcription bias.
