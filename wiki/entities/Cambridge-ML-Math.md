# Cambridge University Mathematics of Machine Learning

**Mathematics of Machine Learning** is an advanced curriculum at the University of Cambridge focusing on the rigorous mathematical foundations, optimization theory, empirical process theory, and statistical mechanics of machine learning algorithms.

## Key Theoretical Modules
- **Nonparametric Estimation & Approximation**: Histogram classifiers, kernel density estimation, and reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces (RKHS).
- **High-Dimensional Statistics**: Sparse linear models, compressed sensing, Lasso ($L_1$), Ridge ($L_2$), and matrix factorizations.
- **Convex & Non-Convex Optimization**: Gradient descent, stochastic approximations, and sub-gradient calculus.
- **Ensemble & Boosting Theory**: AdaBoost, margin theory, and gradient tree boosting.
