# TP53 (Cellular Tumor Antigen p53)

**TP53** is the primary tumor suppressor gene in humans, located on chromosome 17p13.1 (`NC_000017.11`). It encodes the p53 transcription factor, which regulates cell cycle arrest, DNA repair, apoptosis, and senescence in response to cellular stress.

## Genomic & Clinical Significance
- **Canonical Transcript**: `NM_000546.6` (2,629 bp mRNA).
- **Mutational Hotspots in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**: Missense mutations frequently cluster within the DNA-binding core domain (exons 5–8), with hotspot codons including R175, G245, R248, R273, and R282.
- **Exon 5 Benchmark**: Codons 157–193 form the standard test dataset in the [[Genomic-Analyzer]] to test point mutation / SNP detection algorithms.
