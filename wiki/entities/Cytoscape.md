# Cytoscape

**Cytoscape** is the premier open-source software platform for visualizing complex molecular interaction networks and biological pathways.

## Key Plugins & Analysis Modules
- **`CytoHubba`**: Computes 11 topological algorithms (Degree, Edge Percolated Component, Maximum Neighborhood Component, Density of Maximum Neighborhood Component, Maximal Clique Centrality [MCC], Bottleneck, EcCentricity, Closeness, Radiality, Betweenness, Stress) to identify critical hub genes.
- **`MCODE`**: Clusters large networks to discover densely connected functional complexes.
- **`BiNGO` / `ClueGO`**: Gene Ontology mapping directly onto visual graph clusters.
