# Biopython

**Biopython** is the standard open-source Python library for computational molecular biology and bioinformatics.

## Core Modules
- **`Bio.Seq` & `Bio.SeqIO`**: Sequence manipulation, transcription, translation, and I/O for FASTA/GenBank formats.
- **`Bio.Entrez`**: Programmatic access to NCBI E-utilities (PubMed, GenBank, dbSNP).
- **`Bio.PDB`**: Parsing, superimposing, and analyzing 3D coordinates from PDB and mmCIF files.
- **`Bio.Align` & `Bio.AlignIO`**: Multiple sequence alignment (ClustalW, PHYLIP, Stockholm formats).
