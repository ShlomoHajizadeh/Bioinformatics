## 10-Week Study Schedule

### 📚 Weeks 1–2: Foundation
**Daily: 1.5–2 hours**

**Listening (30 min/day)**
- Cambridge IELTS 13, Test 1, Section 1–2 (easy sections)
- Practice: predict answers before listening
- Focus on: spelling, number recognition, map/diagram completion

**Reading (45 min/day)**
- Read 1 IELTS Academic passage per day (650–900 words)
- Practice question types: True/False/Not Given, Matching Headings, Sentence Completion
- Use scientific texts (Nature, PubMed abstracts) for extra practice — directly relevant to your field

**Writing (30 min/day)**
- Task 1 only: practice describing 3 graphs/charts from Cambridge IELTS books
- Template structure:
  ```
  Introduction (paraphrase) → Overview (2 main trends) → Body 1 → Body 2
  ```

**Speaking (15 min/day)**
- Record yourself answering Part 1 questions (home, studies, hobbies)
- Focus on: fluency over perfection, academic vocabulary

---

### 📗 Weeks 3–4: Skill Building
**Daily: 2 hours**

**Listening**
- Cambridge IELTS 14–15, Sections 3–4 (academic lectures)
- Focus: multiple choice, matching, flow chart completion
- **Bioinformatics advantage**: Section 4 monologues are often academic — you understand the register

**Reading**
- 2 passages per session
- Timed practice: 20 minutes per passage
- Master: Matching Information, Yes/No/Not Given (different from True/False!)

**Writing — Task 2 Structure**
```
Paragraph 1: Introduction (2 sentences — rephrase question + state your position)
Paragraph 2: Point 1 + Evidence + Explanation (100-120 words)
Paragraph 3: Point 2 + Evidence + Explanation (100-120 words)
Paragraph 4: Counter-argument + Rebuttal (optional, 60-80 words)
Paragraph 5: Conclusion (2-3 sentences)
Total: 280-320 words
```

**Speaking — Part 2 Long Turn**
- Practice 1 cue card per day (speak for 2 minutes)
- Example topics: "Describe a scientific discovery that interests you"
  → Perfect opportunity to discuss TNBC research, AlphaFold, phylogenetics

---

### 📘 Weeks 5–6: Academic Vocabulary
**Daily: 2 hours + vocabulary**

**Vocabulary (20 min/day)**
Build an IELTS Academic Word List focused on your domain:

| IELTS AWL Word | Bioinformatics Context |
|----------------|----------------------|
| constitute | "These genes constitute the core regulatory network..." |
| demonstrate | "The results demonstrate a significant correlation..." |
| establish | "To establish baseline expression levels..." |
| identify | "We identified 47 differentially expressed genes..." |
| indicate | "pLDDT scores indicate structural confidence..." |
| interpret | "These findings can be interpreted as..." |
| obtain | "Sequence data was obtained from NCBI Entrez..." |
| significant | "A statistically significant difference (p < 0.05)..." |

**Writing — Cambridge grading criteria (each 25%)**
1. **Task Achievement** — fully answer the question
2. **Coherence & Cohesion** — logical flow, paragraphing, connectives
3. **Lexical Resource** — varied, precise vocabulary; avoid repetition
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** — complex sentences, mixed tenses

### 📙 Weeks 7–8: Full Mock Tests
**Every 3 days: 1 complete IELTS Academic test (3 hours)**

Mock test schedule:
- Week 7, Day 1: Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 1 (timed, exam conditions)
- Week 7, Day 4: Cambridge IELTS 16, Test 2
- Week 8, Day 1: Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 1
- Week 8, Day 4: Cambridge IELTS 17, Test 2

**After each mock test:**
- Check answers, note error patterns
- Rewrite Writing tasks based on model answers
- Record ### 📕 Weeks 9–10: Exam Simulation
**Focus: Speed, accuracy, exam technique**

**Listening tips for 7.5+:**
- Read questions during the example/pause — 30 seconds is enough to preview 10 questions
- Never leave a blank — educated guess always
- Transfer answers carefully (10 minutes at end)

**Reading tips for 7.0+:**
- Skim passage for 2-3 minutes before questions
- True/False/NG: find the specific sentence, verify word by word
- Time allocation: 20 min / 20 min / 20 min for 3 passages

**Writing tips for 7.0:**
- Task 1: minimum 150 words, spend 18 minutes
- Task 2: minimum 250 words, spend 38 minutes
- Reserve 4 minutes to proofread both tasks

**Speaking tips for 7.0:**
- Part 3: extend answers with "Furthermore...", "In contrast to...", "From a scientific perspective..."
- Use hedging language: "It could be argued that...", "This may be related to..."
- Don't memorize scripts — examiners detect them

## Speaking — Cambridge Master's (MPhil) Interview Preparation (Dual Purpose)

These IELTS Speaking Part 3 topics directly overlap with Master's interview questions:

| IELTS Topic | Your Answer (bioinformatics context) |
|------------|-------------------------------------|
| "How is technology changing science?" | RNA-seq + AI/AlphaFold2 in cancer biology |
| "Should research be published openly?" | Open access, PLOS ONE, reproducibility |
| "What challenges do scientists face?" | Data quality, statistical assumptions, reproducibility crisis |
| "How do computers help in medicine?" | DESeq2 differential expression, ML classifiers for TNBC subtypes |

> **Strategy**: Practice these answers simultaneously for IELTS and Cambridge supervisor/admissions interviews. One stone, two birds.
